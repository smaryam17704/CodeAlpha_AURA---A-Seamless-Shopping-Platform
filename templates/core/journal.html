Notes on Material, Craft, and Slow Design

Short reads from the AURA studio on the ideas behind what we carry.

Materials

Why we chose vegetable-tanned leather over chrome-tanned

Chrome tanning is faster and cheaper, but it produces a flatter, more uniform hide with a shorter working life. Vegetable tanning — a process largely unchanged for centuries — takes weeks rather than days, but the result ages into something richer with every year of use. It's slower, and that's the point.

Craft

Inside the workshop making our stoneware

Every piece in our ceramics range is thrown on a slow wheel by a small studio that has worked in stoneware for three generations. No two pieces are identical — the slight variation is a signature of hand work, not a flaw to be engineered away.

Philosophy

Buying less, and buying it well

Extraordinary doesn't have to mean loud. It can mean a coat you wear for a decade, or a mug you reach for every morning without thinking. That's the kind of extraordinary AURA is built around.
